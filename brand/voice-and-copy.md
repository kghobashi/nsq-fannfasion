# Voice and copy

- Brand promise: Designed for Confidence. Engineered for Comfort.
- Tagline: Support in Style.
- Both approved lines belong in the brand system; neither replaces the other.
- Preserve the order: confidence is designed for; comfort is engineered for.
- Voice is bold, direct and confident without aggression or crude expression.
- Use concise, photography-led copy; avoid unsupported performance claims and generic luxury superlatives.
- Do not use erotic-themed positioning for the current launch or website.

## Copy hierarchy

Use the promise as the principal statement of what the brand offers and the tagline as its short signature. Both should appear deliberately in the overall site, not necessarily stacked together in every small component. Exact placement is part of concept design.

## Superseded copy

The older phrase “Designed for Comfort. Engineered for Confidence.” is superseded by the founder's 28 September 2026 instruction. Do not restore it from database or email-template records.

“Support in Style” remains approved, now explicitly alongside the revised promise.
