# Philosophy

- Confidence is the central experience the brand should enable.
- Comfort must be expressed through the actual garment, fit and construction as well as the brand promise.
- Fashion and intimate apparel should reinforce personal identity and how the wearer feels.
- Product facts must support published claims about performance, materials or manufacturing.

These principles preserve the documented portfolio role and approved promise. They do not establish new technology, health benefits, environmental credentials or manufacturing capability.
