# FANN FASHION — LEGACY: SPARTAN
## Circle of Ten / Chapter I of X — Cinematic Storyboard v2.1
Updated: 2026-11-12
Status: **Creative direction approved; preproduction working shot list**
Runtime: **approximately 92 seconds**. Master widescreen **2.39:1**, plan 30/15/6s derivatives, vertical-safe framing. Dialogue: none initially. Original score and original visual design.

### Brand and story foundations
- Product promise: **Designed for Confidence. Engineered for Comfort.** Also **Support in Style.** The underwear supports and boosts a man's existing confidence; does not create identity from nothing.
- Hero campaign line: **Their Battles. Your Legacy.** Emotional signature: **Never Stand Alone.** Philosophy: **The Battle Changes. The Spirit Remains.**
- The first garment of the day is the foundation: after shower (no nudity) a drawer of neatly organised FANN underwear, selecting one Spartan pair, shirt buttons, suit/blazer assembly.
- Historical battlefield and modern preparation intercut; ancient Spartans fight overwhelming odds, with no graphic violence. Executive confident throughout.
- **Trigger** is a rear-facing shot of the executive fastening his charcoal jacket button while approaching his home door. The door opens into darkness; the image fades to black (do not show a literal foot crossing the threshold). Return to the battlefield: a Spartan lifts his head, senses a call, warriors stop, turn and march toward lens. The camera pulls backward as the same apparent continuous shot combines battlefield, Spartans, the already seated executive, and finally the entire boardroom.
- **Visual metamorphosis:** crimson capes/helmet crests darken into charcoal/black as two worlds merge; bronze accents retained. Only Spartans, no other warrior tribes in first chapter.
- In the boardroom, Spartan brotherhood is symbolic and non-intimidating; protagonist sits at the head of the table, looks up and gives restrained knowing grin.
- Product hero reveal then the Circle of Ten: Spartan position illuminated, nine concealed. Later chapters explore other real-life challenges beyond executive work, e.g. sport, craftsmanship, creativity, caregiving, entrepreneurship.
- Maintain distinction between inspiration and copying: original armour, choreography, music, VFX and imagery; no proprietary scenes/audio from 300, StarCraft or Iron Man.

### Cinematography and unified colour grade (v2.1)
- Ancient: jewel-like elegant **ruby #7E1429**, shimmering bronze **#AD7642**, amber highlight **#D0A46D**, smoke **#27201D**; no bright blood red and no gratuitous gore.
- Modern: deep black **#111419**, charcoal **#252A30**, graphite **#47484A**, warm bronze **#9C7B61**. Preserve shadow detail, don't crush everything to black.
- Both worlds: same high-contrast cinematic treatment, restrained saturation, sculptural directional lighting, warm rim lighting, black floor and amber highlights. Colour contrast with consistent grading.
- Primary angles: true frontal and true rear-facing third-person symmetrical composition; occasional strict 90-degree side-profile match-cuts. Avoid gratuitous shaky movement and generic 3/4 angles.
- Key transition: **ONE APPARENT CONTINUOUS DOLLY PULLBACK** of Spartans walking toward camera; ancient battlefield gradually resolves into architectural shadows, ruby red capes and helmet crests smoothly change into charcoal black, protagonist seated at head of boardroom comes into view as camera retreats, then reveal wider boardroom and executives. Spartans take position behind protagonist. Plan seamless VFX stitch points; not necessarily one physical take.
- Hold completed wide tableau for 2+ seconds before close-up of protagonist's subtle confident grin. No slogan over the wide tableau; typography comes after his expression.
- The sequence's current beat timings are indicative; adjust shot boundaries in animatic to protect uninterrupted world-merging motion.

### Production rules
- Wardrobe: black/charcoal impeccably tailored suit, dark modern architectural interiors, historical bronze/red; subtle material reference between Spartan motif on garment and armour.
- Editing: violent/noisy ↔ silent/precise first act; rear-facing doorway fade to black at 00:35–00:40; slow ceremonial Spartan turn/march; continuous pullback merge at approximately 00:49–01:15; quiet product finish.
- Shoot garment clean and clearly enough to communicate category without nudity; platform ad variants must pass relevant advertising policies.
- Don't assert audience CTR will be near 100%; measure view-through, brand recall, qualified clicks, product-page engagement and conversion.
- Before production: approve actual product design, casting, location, armour language, VFX test for colour transition and editorial animatic; timings are targets, not locked frame-accurate cuts.
