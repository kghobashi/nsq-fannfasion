## Product family
1. Nominal 20-inch cabin trolley, four twin-wheel casters.
2. Horizontal laptop briefcase, designed to sit on the trolley with a rear pass-through.
3. Structured weekender with softly squared silhouette, padded twin handles and detachable shoulder strap; preserve the original sporty/refined character rather than a cylindrical duffle.
4. Crossbody bag.
5. Laptop backpack.
6. Wallet.

Three complete main colourways: charcoal black, steel blue and ivory white. Each changes the entire body and matching leather, not just stitching. Cognac accents apply across all three. Charcoal is the representative final overview colour. Six product types in three colours implies 18 potential colour/product combinations, not 18 approved manufacturing SKUs.

## Design direction
- Dense practical matte technical textile / nylon, with soft smooth colour-matched leather handles, upper compartment panels and corner reinforcement patches.
- Retain the shaped leather upper panel above each main frontal compartment, carrying the stitched logo. Do not replace these leather panels with patterned textile.
- Rounded rectangular front compartment, one additional horizontal front-pocket zipper, shaped corner patches: sporty detail with visual restraint.
- Fine saddle-cognac piping and selective precise contrast topstitching, inspired by premium sports-car upholstery. Refine cognac away from overly orange thread in samples.
- Preferred latest textile exploration combines fine modern tweed-like texture with restrained vertical hairline pinstripes. Plain charcoal, standalone pinstripe and modern herringbone alternatives were also positively received; final fabric is unresolved.
- Subtle dark metal hardware, no distressed vintage patina, decorative script or conspicuous monograms.

## Branding decision
Option B is preferred: compact charcoal / main-colour leather badge with cognac embroidered FANN, outlined with fine cognac stitching, on the wider smooth leather upper compartment panel.
Badge size, proportions and placement still require refinement. Render lettering is a concept approximation; use actual approved FANN artwork and Bank Gothic display identity for production.

## Proportion and practical details still open
- Trolley must look taller and more substantial relative to laptop briefcase than in early renders; preserve nominal 20-inch cabin concept rather than reverting to a 28-inch check-in case.
- Establish actual exterior dimensions including wheels, handles and filled pockets before selecting airline compatibility targets.
- Final weights, capacities, laptop fit, fabric composition, colour standards, stitching specification, waterproofing, abrasion/pilling/colour-transfer performance, hardware, repairability and production costs are unvalidated.
- Crossbody, backpack and wallet are newly added conceptual extensions; their interiors, sizing and ergonomics have not been developed.
- No market size, customer demand or profitability was validated by the visual review.
