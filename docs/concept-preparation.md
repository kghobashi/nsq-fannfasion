# Next phase: website concept direction

The consolidated foundation and Shopify content structure now provide a specific brief. Use brand context 1.1.0, the supplied logo, exact promise/tagline, Bank Gothic and Montserrat. All garment images remain development concepts.

Prepare three directions after this structure phase: each should show a desktop and mobile homepage composition using the same real collection architecture and copy hierarchy. Explore differences in proportion, image treatment and editorial rhythm, rather than inventing new product lines or changing the brand.

A strong candidate should balance quiet everyday essentials with more expressive Black and Legacy moments. Carry the architectural precision of the garments without turning the whole brand into costume armour. Use gold as a restrained accent; do not make stitched trim appear emissive unless clearly an image-lighting treatment.

The homepage needs an immediate promise, clear routes to five collection families, concise philosophy, a Legacy moment and an appropriate development-stage invitation. Seasonal Editions stays out until its images are reviewed. No X Edition content.

Website concepts come before the native Shopify theme skeleton. Individual garment-image refinement follows selection of the website direction, as requested. In that later phase, correct labels, harmonize colourways and construction, complete Gold's missing individual colours, establish consistent hero/alternate/detail views and refine physical plausibility.

Keep interface typography, the logo and copy editable in implementation; generated website images are references, not flattened production pages.
