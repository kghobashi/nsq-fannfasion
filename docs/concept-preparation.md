# Website concept preparation

Status: proposed preparation brief. No website concept has been selected.

## Fixed inputs

Use the current brand palette, Bank Gothic with Montserrat, the approved promise and tagline, and the current exclusion of erotic-themed underwear. Legacy Line is the retained line. Carry the exact customer action only after choosing the website mode.

## Minimum visual inputs

Establish the main garment type and silhouette, fabric appearance, waistband and logo placement, available colours, and a clear benefit supported by product facts. Obtain at least one accurate garment reference or technical drawing before detailed product renders.

## Next visual deliverable

Prepare three materially distinct directions within the same brand foundation. For each, show a desktop composition, a corresponding mobile composition, a garment or material treatment, and a clear next action. Compare how the direction communicates confidence, comfort and fashion identity.

Keep logo and text editable in the eventual design. Image-generated lettering is not production typography. Preserve deliberate crops and negative space for real copy. Every generated image must have a purpose and an approval status.

## Minimum asset family after selection

| Asset | Required information |
|---|---|
| Desktop/mobile hero | Composition, image crop, text-safe area and intended next action |
| Product front/back/detail | Accurate garment construction, colour, branding and material |
| Material close-up | Realistic texture tied to an actual approved material |
| Fit/size illustration | Verified measurements and fit language |
| Collection image | Confirmed products and distinction within the retained line |
| Packaging | Approved packaging design when available |

Record provenance, rights, dimensions, crop intent, intended use and approval for every production asset. The existing parent-portfolio concept supplies mood; its constellation and parent navigation are not automatically transferred to FANN.
